# UX audit: spanish-elections-2026  2026-10-31  mode: deep
Commit: de72e49  Auditor model: Codex (runtime model identifier not exposed)  Evidence: screenshots at 375, 768 and 1440 px; expanded results at 320 px; source; caller-supplied live interaction evidence

## Verdict
The comparison flow reaches results, but reviewing answers and consulting the methodology can interrupt or erase completed work. The site reads as a plain civic questionnaire, with one verified generic tell and partial adherence to Renzo’s technical-notebook signature. The biggest issue is that an ordinary visit to the methodology followed by “Back to the tool” discards the questionnaire.

## Scores
- Generic-AI score: 3 (distinctive), tells counted: A1-font.
- Signature: partial; concept in one sentence: a neutral civic questionnaire with expandable evidence, rather than a technical notebook.
- Defects: critical 0, high 3, medium 5, low 2.

## Top 5 to fix first
1. Preserve the questionnaire while users consult methodology and election information (D-001).
2. Add explicit “Next” navigation for new and previously selected answers (D-002, D-003).
3. Make expanded topic results fit a 320 px viewport without widening the document (D-005).
4. Make restarting undoable or require confirmation before clearing responses (D-004).
5. Translate editorial explanations in English results and identify original-language passages (D-006).

## Defects (Part C)

### [high] D-001 Consulting the methodology discards questionnaire progress
- Where: `site/index.html:18`, `site/index.html:19`, `site/js/app.js:7`, `site/metodologia.html:91`, `site/metodologia.html:154`; `navigation-loss-1440.png` @ 1440 px, main panel; `navigation-loss.txt`.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Nielsen — user control and freedom; error prevention.
- Problem: Answers exist only in the current document’s memory. Header links navigate away in the same tab, and the methodology’s return link creates a new questionnaire at the introduction. The caller reproduced this after completing answers. The privacy statement explains that answers are not stored, but does not explain that consulting a normal navigation link loses the current session.
- Fix: Keep reference content within the questionnaire document, or open explicitly labelled reference tabs while retaining the original tool. Explain any remaining progress-loss boundary before users cross it; this can be fixed without storing political responses.

### [high] D-002 Changing a radio selection unexpectedly advances the questionnaire
- Where: `site/js/app.js:330`, `site/js/app.js:335`, `site/js/app.js:377`; `question-375.png` @ 375 px, answer controls and navigation; `question-flow.txt`.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: WCAG 2.2, 3.2.2 On Input.
- Problem: Selecting an answer immediately replaces the question and moves focus. No instruction warns about this change of context. The caller reproduced ArrowRight on a focused answer advancing to the following statement, so normal radio-group navigation also submits the answer.
- Fix: Let radio selection update the current answer without navigation. Add an explicit “Next” button that commits the selection and advances.

### [high] D-003 Previously selected answers cannot be retained while advancing
- Where: `site/js/app.js:116`, `site/js/app.js:122`, `site/js/app.js:137`, `site/js/app.js:357`, `site/js/app.js:362`, `site/js/app.js:379`; `edit-same-answer-375.png` @ 375 px, selected answer and bottom navigation; `edit-same-answer.txt`.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Nielsen — user control and freedom; flexibility and efficiency of use.
- Problem: “Change answers” returns to the first question with its answer checked. Clicking that checked answer fires no change event, and the page has no “Next” control. “Skip” advances by replacing the saved answer with `null`. Keeping an answer while reviewing therefore requires changing it, returning, and restoring it.
- Fix: Add “Next” that preserves the current answer, distinguish skipping from keeping an answer, and provide a direct return to results during editing.

### [medium] D-004 Restart clears all responses without confirmation or undo
- Where: `site/js/app.js:225`, `site/js/app.js:366`; `results-375.png` @ 375 px, final action panel.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Nielsen — error prevention; user control and freedom.
- Problem: “Start again” immediately deletes answers and resets province and weights. A mistaken activation loses the work required to answer 30 statements, with no recovery action.
- Fix: Offer a concrete confirmation naming what will be cleared, or retain the previous in-memory session until the user dismisses an “Undo restart” action.

### [medium] D-005 Expanded topic results widen the page at 320 px
- Where: `site/css/style.css:106`, `site/css/style.css:112`, `site/js/app.js:265`; `results-expanded-320.png` @ 320 px, first party’s topic table; `layout-metrics.json`.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Responsive layout; WCAG 2.2, 1.4.10 Reflow.
- Problem: The expanded topic table combines a fixed-width bar with nonwrapping result cells. At a 320 px viewport, the measured document width is 371 px and the table extends to that edge. The document requires horizontal scrolling rather than containing the table’s overflow.
- Fix: Give topic results a deliberate narrow-screen layout: stack each topic’s label, score and coverage, or use flexible bars and a contained scrolling region where necessary. Keep the document width within the viewport.

### [medium] D-006 English results retain untranslated explanatory passages
- Where: `site/js/app.js:282`, `site/js/app.js:291`, `site/js/app.js:301`; `results-expanded-english-375.png` @ 375 px, explanations beneath individual statements.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: WCAG 2.2, 3.1.2 Language of Parts; Nielsen — match between system and the real world.
- Problem: Switching to English translates the interface and statements, but editorial position notes remain in Spanish or Catalan. These explanations contain qualifications needed to interpret the score. Their language is not identified in the markup, so assistive technology also inherits the document’s English pronunciation.
- Fix: Localize editorial notes and vote explanations. Preserve original quotations where appropriate, mark their language, and provide a translated explanation of their relevance.

### [medium] D-007 Topic coverage fractions have no labels or column headers
- Where: `site/js/app.js:249`, `site/js/app.js:265`; `results-expanded-1440.png` @ 1440 px, first party’s “Por tema” table.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: WCAG 2.2, 1.3.1 Info and Relationships; Nielsen — recognition rather than recall.
- Problem: Topic rows display fractions such as `1/3` and `5/5` without explaining their numerator or denominator. The table has a caption and row headers, but no column headers identifying agreement and coverage.
- Fix: Add column headers with `scope="col"` and a concise coverage explanation, such as “Documented positions / your answered statements.” Ensure the narrow-screen presentation retains those labels.

### [medium] D-008 The promised correction route is not linked
- Where: `site/metodologia.html:24`, `site/metodologia.html:90`, `site/metodologia.html:96`, `site/metodologia.html:153`; `methodology-1440.png` @ 1440 px, introduction and “Errores y correcciones”; `methodology-english-1440.png` @ 1440 px, corresponding sections.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Nielsen — help and documentation; trust and verifiability.
- Problem: The methodology tells readers to review the public repository and open an issue, but provides neither a repository link nor an issue link. A reader arriving directly at the site cannot follow the advertised correction process.
- Fix: Link the public repository and a clearly labelled correction issue form from both language versions.

### [low] D-009 Coverage-table failures become an unexplained dash
- Where: `site/js/page.js:33`, `site/js/page.js:60`, `site/metodologia.html:158`.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Nielsen — visibility of system status; help users recognize and recover from errors.
- Problem: If the methodology’s data request or processing fails, its loading placeholder is replaced with `—`. That gives no indication that coverage failed to load or how to recover.
- Fix: Render a localized error message with a retry control, and identify it as a status or alert appropriate to the failure.

### [low] D-010 Page metadata omits sharing and identity assets
- Where: `site/index.html:3`, `site/metodologia.html:3`, `site/eleccion.html:3`.
- Status: verified.
- Standard: Part C6 — metadata and link-preview basics.
- Problem: The document heads have no explicit favicon, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata or Twitter-card metadata. The two reference pages also lack descriptions. Shared links have no authored preview image or complete page-specific metadata.
- Fix: Add page-specific descriptions and canonical URLs, a neutral custom favicon, and explicit social-preview metadata with a designed election-tool image.

## Generic tells (Part A)

### [H] T-001 A1-font — default system sans is the sole typeface
- Where / Evidence: verified — `site/css/style.css:38`; `home-1440.png` @ 1440 px, headline and body; `weights-1440.png` @ 1440 px, heading, topic labels and controls. The rendered screenshots use the same system-sans treatment throughout; no authored font is loaded. The exact platform-resolved family was not measured.
- Why it reads generic: Typography contributes no subject-specific identity beyond conventional size and weight changes. This is one strong tell, not evidence of a wider template cluster.
- Fix: Deliberately load a display monospace for headings, labels and numbers, paired with a neutral body sans. Preserve the current readable body size and line spacing.

## Missing signature (Part B)

### S-001 Item 2 — the specified display/body type pairing is absent
- Observation: verified, medium — `site/css/style.css:38`, `site/css/style.css:56`; `home-1440.png` @ 1440 px, headline; `results-1440.png` @ 1440 px, party headings and numbers. Renzo’s signature specifies deliberately loaded monospace display typography and a separate body face; neither appears here.
- Direction: Choose a mono/sans pairing such as IBM Plex Mono with IBM Plex Sans, or JetBrains Mono with a restrained body sans. Apply the mono first to headings, progress labels and result numerals.

### S-002 Item 3 — the palette differs from the notebook specification
- Observation: verified, medium — `site/css/style.css:3`, `site/css/style.css:8`, `site/css/style.css:19`; `home-1440.png` @ 1440 px, background and primary action; `weights-375.png` @ 375 px, selected weights. The site uses near-grey paper, white cards and blue controls; the signature specifies warm paper, warm ink and sparse signal orange. Dark-mode neutrals are also untinted greys.
- Direction: Introduce warmer paper and ink tokens with a restrained shared accent, or document a project-specific neutral-blue signature that explicitly overrides the global specification. Keep party comparison bars neutral; no partisan colour coding is required.

### S-003 Items 1 and 4 — composition does not express a technical notebook
- Observation: verified, medium — `site/css/style.css:49`, `site/css/style.css:54`, `site/css/style.css:55`; `home-1440.png`, `question-1440.png` and `methodology-1440.png` @ 1440 px, main content. Pages repeat the same centred column and bordered card. Text is left-aligned, but there are no margin notes, varied reading widths or asymmetric information relationships.
- Direction: Keep the questionnaire compact while giving desktop reference pages a narrow annotation column; alternatively, place source coverage and methodological caveats alongside the results as notebook-style margin notes. Establish this structure with the existing content rather than adding decorative sections.
